# Job: Material-aware texture measures and PBR value rules

The judge's UV and albedo measures read UV map 0 and raw texel values, ignoring which UV map and transform the material actually samples (`texCoord`, `KHR_texture_transform`) and `baseColorFactor`; texture slots count images that a Mix factor of 0 or a multiply by 0 keeps from reaching the render; albedo pools texels from tiny or hidden faces without area weighting; and a triangle spanning many UV tiles can blow up the albedo rasteriser. There are also no rules on PBR plausibility, which engines and artists rely on: albedo within roughly sRGB 30 to 240, metallic near 0 or 1, roughness in [0, 1], a stated normal-map convention, no lighting baked into albedo.

## Base

- **Base commit:** the `main` commit this spec is added in. Rebase onto the latest `main` before delivering.

## Required

1. **Material-aware measures:** UV coverage, overlap and albedo statistics follow each texture to the UV map and mapping/transform its material actually uses and apply `baseColorFactor` (or refuse non-identity transforms for a rule, documented); texture-slot rules count only images that reach the shader output (a constant-zero Mix factor or multiply stops the traversal); albedo is weighted by 3D area over UV area on visible faces only; rasterising clips triangles per tile so tiled islands cannot exhaust memory.
2. **`pbr` spec group:**
   - `albedoRange` (default sRGB 30 to 240 for non-emissive dielectrics, metals allowed brighter), as the share of visible-area texels outside the range;
   - `metallicBinary` (share of visible-area texels with metallic between 0.1 and 0.9);
   - `roughnessRange`;
   - `normalConvention` (`opengl` or `directx`), checked by the sign of the green-channel response on a known bevel or by the asset's declared convention, stated how;
   - `bakedLighting`: a measure of lighting baked into albedo (for example correlation between albedo luminance and a fixed-light rendered shading term, or between albedo and ambient occlusion beyond what a cavity-darkening material explains), calibrated on Blendworks' own procedural and baked assets and on CC0 scans, made a default only if it separates;
   - `seams`: luminance or normal discontinuity across UV seams on the rendered or reconstructed surface.
   Each documented with its source or calibration.
3. **Equivalence:** existing proof assets and fixtures keep their verdicts; changed values are listed and justified, and re-recorded only where the measure changed.

## Tests

One negative test per acceptance item: a test that fails when the behaviour the item names stops holding. Say how you proved it, for example by breaking the code once and watching the test fail. Include crafted fixtures: a clean UV0 with an overlapping UV1 that the material samples; a texture transform scaled near zero; a near-black `baseColorFactor` over a noisy texture; an image muted by a Mix factor 0; albedo with a baked light gradient; metallic at 0.5 everywhere; a DirectX normal map declared as OpenGL.
