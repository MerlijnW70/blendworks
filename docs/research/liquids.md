# Liquids: sources of the physical values

Job `liquids`. Every number in `bridge/blendworks_bridge/liquid_presets.py`, `liquid_spectrum.py`, `liquid_light.py`, `liquid_river.py` and `liquid_extras.py` comes from the sources below, each under the key the preset's `sources` list names. Where a value is derived (a unit conversion, a dilution, an extrapolation) the derivation is given; where only a typical value exists and no measurement was found, the entry says **estimate**, so a reader knows which numbers are measured and which are not.

Conventions: absorption `a` and scattering `b` are per metre for the red, green and blue channel, read at 600, 550 and 450 nm. A decadic absorbance `A` over a path `d` becomes `a = A ln 10 / d`. The Narasimhan and Jensen tables are in mm^-1 and are multiplied by 1000.

## Wind from the weather

`weather` keeps no wind speed. A liquid takes the drift direction of the lowest cloud layer (weather sets its low layers to 95°, the compass direction the wind blows towards) and a speed from the storm value whose layers match the stored ones: 2 m/s clear, 3.5 fair cumulus, 5 scattered, 6.5 broken, 7.5 overcast, 8.5 light rain, 11 heavy rain, 14 thunderstorm. These follow the descriptions of the Beaufort scale (force 2–3 light and gentle breeze in fair weather, force 5–6 with rain fronts, force 7 gusts under thunderstorms; WMO Manual on Codes, WMO-No. 306, Beaufort table) and are a **heuristic** mapping, not a measurement. A call's own `wind` overrides both.
